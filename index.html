Who we are

One partner for design, delivery and compliance across the energy lifecycle.

Established in 2017, Subseazen has grown from API design and consulting into the delivery of complex, risk-intensive and multidisciplinary projects — manufacturing, inspection, repair, maintenance, audit, consulting, manpower supply and safety-equipment support.

We combine design capability, field execution, quality management and practical oilfield service experience to support engineering, inspection, repair, recertification and safety scopes end to end.

More about us
  • Standards-led. Design and documentation aligned to API 6A, 16C, 17D, 4F and 8C.
  • Quality assured. ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system.
  • Global reach. Offices in the UK, UAE and India with field execution worldwide.
  • 24 × 7 support. Planned and emergency response with fast off-site turnaround.
