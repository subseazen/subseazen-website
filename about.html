Our story

One-stop-shop for the energy lifecycle.

Subseazen Engineering Services Ltd was established in 2017 to deliver engineering services for the Oil & Gas, EPC and Marine industries. The company has grown from API design and consulting into the delivery of complex, risk-intensive and multidisciplinary projects — including manufacturing, inspection, repair, maintenance, audit, consulting, manpower supply and safety-equipment manufacturing and supplier support.

We work with clients large and small, at domestic and international levels, providing cost-effective and timely results across manufacturing & machining, testing & repair, fabrication, raw-material trading, flow-iron products, lined valves and drilling, completion and production equipment — serving the oil & gas, marine, power, chemical and ship-building industries.
